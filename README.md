arm 1 - sink Arm (Datis)
- takes the cup from the starting point and fills it with water
- selector used 
    - water amount 
        - (changes how long the cup is held under the tap, and how big the water object is in the cup)

arm 2 - kettle arm (Louis)
- takes the cup from arm 1 and puts it on the (selected) stove, and takes it off
- selector used 
    - which stove
    - water temperature
        - determines how long the cup is held on the stove
            - also needs to do calculations regarding how much water in cup and whatnot
            - because 10ml of water takes quicker to heat up than 500ml

arm 3 - teabag arm (Arnoe or communal)
- takes the selected tea bag from the box and places it in the teacup
- selector used 
    - which teabag

arm 4 - milk arm (Arnoe or communal)
- takes the selected milk from the spot and pours it into the teacup
- selector used
    - which milk
    - milk amount
        - (changes how long the milk is poured into the cup, and how big the tea object is in the cup)
